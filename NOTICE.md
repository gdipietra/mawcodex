## Adaptation boundary

The 52 source-derived academic workflow skills, 18 source-derived role pairs,
32 research and quality rules, and mapped provider surfaces retain Pedro's
substantive workflow lineage. MAW Codex translates those capabilities into
Codex-native skills, hierarchical `AGENTS.md`, portable roles, bounded
reviewers, explicit authorization gates, opt-in hooks, and auditable
PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED evidence.

ManageRAW and the JAW, CAW, PAW, LAW, UAW, and SAW control-plane design are
original MAW Codex additions by Giovanni Di Pietra. They address safe adoption
in ongoing projects, capability ownership, durable personalization,
instruction layering, update reconciliation, and sanitized project learning.
